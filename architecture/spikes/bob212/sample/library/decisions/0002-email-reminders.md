# Reminders go by email

## Context

Members forget due dates. Toolshed needs to remind them a day before a loan is due, and when it is overdue.

## Options

1. Email.
2. SMS.
3. Push notifications to the web app.

## Decision

Option 1: email through a transactional provider. Every member has an address, since sign-up is by email link.

## Consequences

- Cheap, and it needs nothing on the member's phone.
- Reminders land among other mail and are often missed.
