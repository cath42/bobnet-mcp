# Glossary

## Check-in

Also: return

A steward taking a tool back at the counter. It ends the loan, and the tool is lendable again.

## Check-out

A steward handing a tool to a member at the counter. It starts a loan.

## Damage report

A note that something is wrong with a tool, written by the member or a steward. If the tool needs repair, it leaves
the catalogue until it is fixed.

## Hold

The time a reservation keeps a tool for its member: until 24 hours after the pickup day. After that it lapses and
the tool is free again.

## Loan

A tool in a member's hands, from check-out to check-in. It has a due date.

## Member

Someone signed up to borrow tools. Joining is free. A member with an overdue loan can't borrow another tool.

## Reservation

A member asking for a tool on a given pickup day. It puts the tool on hold.

## Shed

A place where tools are kept and handed over, with opening hours. Every tool lives in one shed.

## Steward

Also: volunteer

A member who runs a shed's counter: checks tools out and in, and writes damage reports.

## Tool

One physical thing on a shelf. Two identical drills are two tools.
